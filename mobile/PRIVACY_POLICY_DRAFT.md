# RabPDF Privacy Policy (draft)

Last updated: October 1, 2026

RabPDF is developed by Nishan Chettri, with assistance from ChatGPT.

RabPDF processes the files you choose locally on your device. The app does not
upload documents, QR links, passwords, or generated files to a RabPDF server.
It does not include accounts, advertising, analytics, or remote crash reporting.

Files selected for processing and operation results are held in app memory.
Copies made for the Android share sheet can remain in the app's cache until
Android removes them or you clear the app's cache. Files you explicitly save
remain in the destination you select and are controlled by that storage provider.

When you choose to share a file, the receiving app handles it under its own
privacy policy. If you open the developer website, your browser connects to that
website under the website's privacy policy. RabPDF's file picker and save picker
allow you to grant access to selected documents without requesting broad access
to all files on your device.

The current app does not need personal information to perform its tools and
does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

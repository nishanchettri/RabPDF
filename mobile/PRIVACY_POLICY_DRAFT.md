# RabPDF Privacy Policy (draft)

Last updated: October 2, 2026

RabPDF is developed by Nishan Chettri, with assistance from ChatGPT.

RabPDF processes the files you choose locally on your device. The app does not
upload documents, QR links, passwords, or generated files to a RabPDF server.
It does not require accounts or include remote PDF processing. All tools are
free. Android ad-enabled builds include Google AdMob and its User Messaging
Platform. Ads use Internet access independently of local PDF processing.
RabPDF does not pass documents, passwords, QR inputs, or generated files to ads.

Google's advertising SDK may collect or share IP addresses, device identifiers,
app interactions, and diagnostic information according to the SDK configuration
and consent choices. Review the exact SDK disclosures before publication:
https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where required, a consent form is shown and an Ad privacy choices control lets
you revisit choices. Advertising and consent availability never restrict PDF
tools. Off-mode builds do not include advertising or consent SDKs. Delete the
inapplicable build-mode description before publishing this policy.

Files selected for processing and operation results are held in app memory.
Copies made for the Android share sheet can remain in the app's cache until
Android removes them or you clear the app's cache. Files you explicitly save
remain in the destination you select and are controlled by that storage provider.

Document/QR camera scanning uses Google Play services' on-device scanner APIs.
Scanner modules can download on first use. Google Play services handles camera
access and its own SDK behavior; review Google's current terms and disclosures.
RabPDF imports scanned images into its local editor and removes its temporary
camera cache copies after import. Failed or interrupted sessions may leave cache
files until Android clears them or the user clears the app cache.

The app retains up to five recently saved file names, document locations, and
access URIs in local app storage. It does not retain document contents in this
history. Clear list removes these entries. Access can expire or files can move.
Signatures and scan edits stay in local memory until the user exports them.
RabPDF does not send scanned images, ID cards, signatures, or QR scan contents
to its advertising integration or a RabPDF server.

When you choose to share a file, the receiving app handles it under its own
privacy policy. If you open the developer website, your browser connects to that
website under the website's privacy policy. RabPDF's file picker and save picker
allow you to grant access to selected documents without requesting broad access
to all files on your device.

The current app does not need personal information to perform its tools and
does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
